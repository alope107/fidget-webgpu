Impulse magnitude:

Change in velocity:
    v_new = v_old + (j * n/mass)
    j = Unscaled Impulse
    n = collision normal

Impulse is a Change in momentum: mass dependent! Each object must depend on its own mass
Naturally more massive objects will be more impacted, less massive will be less impacted
